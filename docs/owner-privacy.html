Wash Up Owner Privacy Policy

Wash Up Owner is the app for owners and managers of car washes registered on Wash Up. This policy explains what the app collects and why. Last updated: November 2, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number and role. Accounts are created by Wash Up or by the wash owner; the app has no sign-up.
  • Business records you add or change: services, prices, offers, schedules, orders, payments collected at the wash, notes and replies to reviews.
  • Staff details you add: name, mobile number and role.
  • A device token, so we can send you notifications about new bookings.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing your wash's orders and reports, and sending notifications. We do not sell data, show ads or track you across other apps.

Sharing

We do not share this data with other companies. Customers of your wash see only what the customer app shows them, such as your wash name, services and prices.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your wash account is active. The owner can deactivate a staff account in the app at any time. To delete your account, a staff account or your wash's data, email bdallhmsd807@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

ما نجمعه

  • بيانات الحساب: الاسم ورقم الموبايل والدور. تُنشأ الحسابات من واش أب أو من صاحب المغسلة، ولا يوجد تسجيل من داخل التطبيق.
